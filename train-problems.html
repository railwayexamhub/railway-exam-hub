Train Problems

RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 13

About this chapter

Train crossing poles, platforms and moving trains. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • Convert km/h to m/s using × 5/18.
  • Time to cross a pole = train length ÷ speed.
  • Time to cross a platform = (train length + platform length) ÷ speed.
  • For two trains, use relative speed.

Important formulas and reminders

Solved examples

Practice concept 1

A 100 m train moves at 10 m/s. Time to cross a pole?

Answer: 10 seconds

Time = 100 ÷ 10 = 10 seconds.

Practice concept 2

A 150 m train crosses a 100 m platform at 25 m/s. Time taken?

Answer: 10 seconds

Total distance = 150 + 100 = 250 m. Time = 250/25 = 10 seconds.

Practice concept 3

Two trains move in opposite directions at 40 and 50 km/h. Relative speed is:

Answer: 90 km/h

Opposite-direction relative speed = 40 + 50 = 90 km/h.
