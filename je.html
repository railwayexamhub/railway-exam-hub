Junior Engineer and related technical post preparation

Preparation overview

Common CBT subjects plus the relevant engineering discipline. Technical topics differ by discipline; use the post-specific notification as the controlling syllabus.

Railway Exam Hub provides a common preparation foundation and will add detailed post-specific chapters, practice sets and mock tests in their relevant sections.

Study areas to build for this exam

Each subject area must be expanded into full-length lessons with concepts, definitions, worked examples, formulas or technical facts where relevant, and distinct practice questions with checked answers and explanations.

Post-specific content will be separated by recruitment category and technical discipline rather than incorrectly applying one exam's syllabus to another.

Important syllabus notice

Recruitment stages, subject weightage, eligibility, post categories and syllabus can change. Confirm the applicable details from the current official recruitment notification before relying on an exam plan.
