BODMAS and Simplification

RRB Group D Mathematics – Chapter 2

1. Introduction

Simplification means reducing a mathematical expression into its simplest form by following the correct order of operations.

BODMAS helps us solve expressions without making mistakes.

2. BODMAS Rule

B – Brackets
O – Orders (Powers and Roots)
D – Division
M – Multiplication
A – Addition
S – Subtraction

Important: Division and multiplication have equal priority. Solve from left to right. Addition and subtraction also have equal priority and are solved from left to right.

3. Types of Brackets

  • ( ) – Small brackets or parentheses
  • { } – Curly brackets
  • [ ] – Square brackets

Solve the innermost brackets first.

4. Important Rules

a + 0 = a
a × 1 = a
a × 0 = 0
a ÷ 1 = a
a - a = 0
a² = a × a

5. Solved Examples

Example 1

Solve: 20 + 12 ÷ 4 × 2

Step 1: Division and multiplication from left to right.

= 20 + 3 × 2

= 20 + 6

Answer = 26

Example 2

Solve: 50 - (12 + 8) ÷ 4

= 50 - 20 ÷ 4

= 50 - 5

Answer = 45

Example 3

Solve: 5² + 24 ÷ 6

= 25 + 4

Answer = 29

Example 4

Solve: 100 ÷ [5 × (2 + 3)]

= 100 ÷ [5 × 5]

= 100 ÷ 25

Answer = 4

7. Quick Revision

  • Always solve brackets first.
  • Solve powers and roots next.
  • Division and multiplication: left to right.
  • Addition and subtraction: left to right.
  • Never solve strictly from left to right when operations have different priorities.
