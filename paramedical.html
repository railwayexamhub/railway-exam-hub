Common CBT pattern and post-specific professional syllabus

Recruitment stages

For CEN 04/2024, recruitment uses a single-stage Computer Based Test (CBT), followed by Document Verification. The RRB may conduct additional CBTs if considered necessary.

Duration: 90 minutes (120 minutes for eligible PwBD candidates using a scribe). Objective multiple-choice questions. Negative marking: 1/3 mark for each incorrect answer.

Common subjects

General Awareness

Current affairs, Indian geography, culture and history, Indian polity and Constitution, Indian economy, environmental issues, sports, and scientific and technological developments.

General Arithmetic, Intelligence & Reasoning

Arithmetic fundamentals and problem solving; number and alphabet series; analogy; coding-decoding; relationships; classification; directions; logical and analytical reasoning. Refer to the detailed notice for the full post-specific outline.

General Science

Physics, Chemistry and Life Sciences at approximately the 10th-standard CBSE level, as stated in the notification.

Professional Ability — choose your notified post

This is the largest section (70 marks). It is not one common syllabus for every paramedical applicant. The professional syllabus depends on the exact post and qualification. Examples of notified categories include Nursing Superintendent, Pharmacist, Laboratory Assistant/Technician, Radiographer/X-Ray Technician, ECG Technician, Dietician, Dental Hygienist, Audiologist and other posts.

Study the professional subject listed for your own post in Annexure-C of CEN 04/2024. Do not prepare Nursing topics for a Pharmacist post, or assume one post's syllabus applies to another.
