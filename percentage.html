Percentage

RRB Group D Mathematics • Chapter 6

About this chapter

Percentage means 'per hundred'. Convert percentages to fractions or decimals to calculate quickly.

Study tip: Read the rules, practise the worked examples, then attempt the quiz without looking at the notes.

1. Key concepts and rules

  • To find p% of a number, multiply the number by p/100.
  • An increase of p% means multiply by (100+p)/100.
  • A decrease of p% means multiply by (100-p)/100.
  • Successive percentage changes are applied one after another, not simply added.

2. Important formulas and reminders

p% = p/100
Percentage = (part ÷ whole) × 100
New value after p% increase = original × (1+p/100)
New value after p% decrease = original × (1-p/100)

3. Solved examples

Example 1: Find 20% of 450

450 × 20/100 = 90.

Answer: 90

Example 2: A value rises from 200 to 250. Find the percentage increase.

Increase = 50. Percentage increase = 50/200 × 100.

Answer: 25%

Example 3: Find 15% of 800

800 × 15/100 = 120.

Answer: 120

4. Exam tips

  • Write down the given values before selecting a formula.
  • Check that units are consistent.
  • Do calculations step by step and simplify the final answer.
  • Estimate the result to catch arithmetic mistakes.
  • Practise questions under a time limit after learning the method.
