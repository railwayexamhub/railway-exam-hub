Percentage

RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 06

About this chapter

Percentage conversion, increase and decrease, successive changes and exam applications. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • Percent means per hundred.
  • x% of y = (x/100) × y.
  • To convert a fraction to a percentage, multiply by 100.
  • Percentage change = (change ÷ original value) × 100.
  • For successive percentage changes, apply each change to the updated value.

Solved examples

Practice concept 1

Find 25% of 240.

Answer: 60

25% = 1/4. One-fourth of 240 is 60.

Practice concept 2

A value rises from 80 to 100. Find the percentage increase.

Answer: 25%

Increase = 20. Percentage increase = 20/80 × 100 = 25%.

Practice concept 3

Convert 3/5 into a percentage.

Answer: 60%

3/5 × 100 = 60%.
