Ratio and Proportion

RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 05

About this chapter

Simplifying ratios, equivalent ratios, division in a ratio and direct proportion. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • A ratio compares quantities in the same units.
  • Simplify a ratio by dividing all terms by their HCF.
  • If a:b = c:d, then ad = bc.
  • To divide a quantity in ratio m:n, total parts = m+n.
  • In direct proportion, both quantities increase or decrease in the same ratio.

Solved examples

Practice concept 1

Simplify 24:36.

Answer: 2:3

Divide both terms by their HCF, 12. The simplified ratio is 2:3.

Practice concept 2

Divide 60 in the ratio 2:3. What is the smaller share?

Answer: 24

Total parts = 5. One part = 60/5 = 12. Smaller share = 2 × 12 = 24.

Practice concept 3

If 4:5 = x:20, find x.

Answer: 16

4/5 = x/20, so x = 20 × 4/5 = 16.
