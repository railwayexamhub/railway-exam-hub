Ratio and Proportion

RRB Group D Mathematics • Chapter 5

About this chapter

A ratio compares quantities in the same units. A proportion states that two ratios are equal.

Study tip: Read the rules, practise the worked examples, then attempt the quiz without looking at the notes.

1. Key concepts and rules

  • Simplify a ratio by dividing every term by the HCF.
  • Convert quantities to the same unit before comparing.
  • If a:b = c:d, then ad = bc.
  • Divide a quantity in the ratio a:b by multiplying the total by a/(a+b) and b/(a+b).

2. Important formulas and reminders

Simplified ratio = a ÷ HCF : b ÷ HCF
If a:b = c:d, then a/b = c/d
Fourth proportional d = (b × c)/a
Direct proportion: x₁/y₁ = x₂/y₂

3. Solved examples

Example 1: Simplify 24:36

Divide both terms by 12.

Answer: 2:3

Example 2: Divide ₹500 in the ratio 2:3

Total parts = 5. Shares are 500×2/5 and 500×3/5.

Answer: ₹200 and ₹300

Example 3: If 4:7 = x:21, find x

x/21 = 4/7, so x = 12.

Answer: 12

4. Exam tips

  • Write down the given values before selecting a formula.
  • Check that units are consistent.
  • Do calculations step by step and simplify the final answer.
  • Estimate the result to catch arithmetic mistakes.
  • Practise questions under a time limit after learning the method.
