Elementary Statistics

RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 18

About this chapter

Mean, median, mode, range and reading simple data. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • Mean = sum of observations ÷ number of observations.
  • Median is the middle value after arranging data in order.
  • Mode is the most frequently occurring value.
  • Range = maximum value − minimum value.
  • For an even number of observations, median is the mean of the two middle values.

Important formulas and reminders

Solved examples

Practice concept 1

Find the mean of 2, 4 and 6.

Answer: 4

Mean = (2+4+6)/3 = 12/3 = 4.

Practice concept 2

Find the median of 3, 7, 9.

Answer: 7

The middle value in the ordered list is 7.

Practice concept 3

Find the mode of 2, 3, 3, 5.

Answer: 3

3 occurs more often than any other value.
