Statistics

RRB Group D Mathematics • Chapter 18

About this chapter

Statistics organises and summarises data using averages and measures of frequency.

Study tip: Read the rules, practise the worked examples, then attempt the quiz without looking at the notes.

1. Key concepts and rules

  • Arrange data in order before finding the median.
  • For an odd number of observations, median is the middle value.
  • For an even number, median is the mean of the two middle values.
  • Mode is the most frequent value.
  • Range = maximum − minimum.

2. Important formulas and reminders

Mean = Sum of observations ÷ Number of observations
Median (odd n) = value at position (n+1)/2
Median (even n) = average of values at positions n/2 and n/2+1
Mode = most frequent observation
Range = Maximum − Minimum

3. Solved examples

Example 1: Find the mean of 2, 4, 6 and 8.

Sum = 20; count = 4.

Answer: 5

Example 2: Find the median of 3, 7, 9, 11 and 15.

The middle (third) value is 9.

Answer: 9

Example 3: Find the range of 12, 5, 19 and 8.

Maximum 19 minus minimum 5.

Answer: 14

4. Exam tips

  • Write down the given values before selecting a formula.
  • Check that units are consistent.
  • Do calculations step by step and simplify the final answer.
  • Estimate the result to catch arithmetic mistakes.
  • Practise questions under a time limit after learning the method.
