RPF Sub-Inspector

CBT syllabus, physical tests and recruitment stages

Recruitment stages

  1. Computer Based Test (CBT)
  2. Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Measurement Test (PMT)
  3. Document Verification (DV)
  4. Medical examination as prescribed

PET is qualifying in nature. Ex-servicemen are exempt from PET but are required to undergo PMT under the notification.

CBT pattern — RPF SI CEN RPF 01/2024

Duration: 90 minutes. Negative marking: 1/3 mark for each incorrect answer. CBT standard: graduation level.

Subject-wise syllabus

1. Arithmetic

Number system, whole numbers, decimals and fractions; relationships between numbers; fundamental arithmetic operations; percentages; ratio and proportion; averages; interest; profit and loss; discount; tables and graphs; mensuration; time and distance.

2. General Intelligence & Reasoning

Analogies; similarities and differences; spatial visualization and orientation; problem solving and analysis; judgment and decision making; visual memory; observation; relationships; arithmetical reasoning; verbal and figure classification; number series; non-verbal series; coding and decoding; statement-conclusion; syllogistic reasoning.

3. General Awareness

Current events; environment and its application to society; everyday observation and experience; Indian history; art and culture; geography; economics; general polity; Indian Constitution; sports; general science.

Physical tests

Qualifying PET and PMT standards differ by sex and category. Check the exact height, chest (where applicable), running and long-jump/high-jump standards in the applicable official notification. Do not use unofficial or generic fitness targets as eligibility standards.
