Pipes and Cisterns

RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 11

About this chapter

Inlet and outlet rates, filling and emptying tanks, and combined pipes. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • An inlet adds water; an outlet removes water.
  • If a pipe fills a tank in x hours, its rate is 1/x tank per hour.
  • For an outlet, use a negative rate.
  • Net rate = sum of inlet rates − sum of outlet rates.
  • Time to fill = 1 ÷ net filling rate.

Solved examples

Practice concept 1

A pipe fills a tank in 4 hours. What fraction does it fill in one hour?

Answer: 1/4

One-hour filling rate is 1/4 of the tank.

Practice concept 2

Two pipes fill a tank in 6 and 3 hours. Together they take:

Answer: 2 hours

Combined rate = 1/6 + 1/3 = 1/2 tank/hour. Time = 2 hours.

Practice concept 3

An inlet fills 1/5 tank per hour and an outlet empties 1/10 per hour. Net rate is:

Answer: 1/10 tank/hour

Net rate = 1/5 − 1/10 = 1/10 tank per hour.
