Fractions and Decimals

RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 04

About this chapter

Types of fractions, equivalent fractions, operations, recurring decimals and conversions. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • To add or subtract fractions, use a common denominator.
  • Multiply fractions by multiplying numerators and denominators.
  • Divide by a fraction by multiplying by its reciprocal.
  • To convert a terminating decimal to a fraction, write it over 10, 100, 1000, etc., then simplify.
  • A recurring decimal can be converted to a fraction using place-value algebra.

Solved examples

Practice concept 1

What is 1/2 + 1/4?

Answer: 3/4

Use denominator 4: 1/2 = 2/4. Then 2/4 + 1/4 = 3/4.

Practice concept 2

Convert 0.75 into a fraction in simplest form.

Answer: 3/4

0.75 = 75/100. Divide numerator and denominator by 25 to get 3/4.

Practice concept 3

Find 2/3 × 3/5.

Answer: 2/5

Multiply: (2 × 3)/(3 × 5). Cancel 3 to obtain 2/5.
