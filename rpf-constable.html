CBT syllabus, physical tests and recruitment stages

Recruitment stages

Computer Based Test (CBT) → Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Measurement Test (PMT) → Document Verification (DV). PET is qualifying in nature; candidates must meet the applicable physical criteria in the official notice.

CBT pattern

SectionQuestions / Marks
General Awareness50
Arithmetic35
General Intelligence & Reasoning35
Total120

Duration: 90 minutes. Marking: +1 for each correct answer; −1/3 for each incorrect answer; no mark change for an unattempted question. The standard is Matriculation / 10th level.

Subject-wise syllabus

Arithmetic (35 marks)

Number system, whole numbers, decimals and fractions, relationships between numbers, fundamental arithmetic operations, percentages, ratio and proportion, averages, interest, profit and loss, discount, tables and graphs, mensuration, time and distance, and related elementary arithmetic.

General Intelligence & Reasoning (35 marks)

Analogies, similarities and differences, spatial visualization and orientation, problem solving, analysis, judgement, decision-making, visual memory, observation, relationship concepts, arithmetical reasoning, verbal and figure classification, number series, non-verbal series, coding-decoding, statement-conclusion and syllogistic reasoning.

General Awareness (50 marks)

Current events and everyday awareness; Indian History, Art and Culture, Geography, Economics, General Polity, Indian Constitution, Sports and General Science.

Physical test preparation

Prepare separately for the prescribed running, long-jump and high-jump requirements, and review the height/chest standards applicable to your category and gender. The official notice specifies different PET/PMT criteria and exemptions; check the relevant table before training to a target.

Passing PET is mandatory and qualifying; it does not add marks to the CBT score.
