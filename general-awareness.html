1. Indian History

  • Harappan Civilization: Harappa discovered 1921 (Daya Ram Sahni); Mohenjo-daro 1922 (R.D. Banerji).
  • Maurya Empire: founded by Chandragupta Maurya (321 BC); Ashoka fought the Kalinga War in 261 BC and embraced Buddhism.
  • Gupta Period: Samudragupta is called the "Napoleon of India"; Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya) had Kalidasa in his court.
  • Delhi Sultanate (1206-1526): Slave, Khilji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi dynasties.
  • Mughals: Babur won the First Battle of Panipat (1526); Akbar won the Second Battle of Panipat (1556); Shah Jahan built the Taj Mahal.
  • British era: Battle of Plassey 1757, Battle of Buxar 1764, Revolt of 1857, INC founded 1885.
  • Freedom movement: Partition of Bengal 1905, Jallianwala Bagh 1919, Non-Cooperation 1920, Dandi March 1930, Quit India 1942, Independence 15 August 1947.
Memory Trick: Delhi Sultanate = "Some Kings Take Some Land" → Slave, Khilji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi.
Mughals = "Bab Hum Ak Jah Shah Aur" → Babur, Humayun, Akbar, Jahangir, Shah Jahan, Aurangzeb.

2. Indian Geography

  • India has 28 states and 8 Union Territories.
  • Largest state by area: Rajasthan. Smallest: Goa. Most populous: Uttar Pradesh.
  • Highest peak in India: Kanchenjunga (8,586 m). Longest river in India: Ganga.
  • Largest desert: Thar. Largest freshwater lake: Wular Lake. Largest brackish lagoon: Chilika.
  • Tropic of Cancer passes through 8 states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, Mizoram.
  • Indian Standard Time is based on 82°30'E longitude, passing near Mirzapur (Uttar Pradesh).
  • Longest dam: Hirakud (Odisha). Highest dam: Tehri (Uttarakhand).
Memory Trick (Tropic of Cancer): "Gujarat Rajasthan Might Chase Jharkhand's West Bengal Tripura Mizoram" → G-R-M-C-J-W-T-M.

3. Indian Polity

  • Constitution adopted on 26 November 1949; came into force on 26 January 1950.
  • Constituent Assembly president: Dr. Rajendra Prasad. Drafting Committee chairman: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar.
  • Originally 395 Articles, 22 Parts and 8 Schedules; now 12 Schedules.
  • Fundamental Rights: Articles 12-35 (six rights). Right to Education: Article 21A.
  • Directive Principles: Articles 36-51. Fundamental Duties: Article 51A (11 duties).
  • President: Article 52. Vice President: Article 63. Parliament = President + Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha.
  • Lok Sabha: maximum 552 members (543 elected). Rajya Sabha: maximum 250 members.
  • Preamble was amended once, by the 42nd Amendment (1976): added "Socialist", "Secular", "Integrity".
Memory Trick (6 Fundamental Rights): "Equal Freedom, Exploits Religion, Culture Courts" → Equality, Freedom, Against Exploitation, Religion, Cultural & Educational rights, Constitutional Remedies.

4. Indian Economy

  • Reserve Bank of India (RBI): established 1 April 1935, headquartered in Mumbai.
  • NITI Aayog replaced the Planning Commission on 1 January 2015.
  • First Five Year Plan: 1951 (agriculture focus).
  • GST was launched on 1 July 2017.
  • Currency symbol: ₹ (adopted 2010). Currency notes are printed under RBI's authority; one-rupee notes are issued by the Ministry of Finance.
  • Primary sector = agriculture; secondary = industry; tertiary = services.

5. Indian Railways GK

  • First passenger train: 16 April 1853, Bori Bunder (Bombay) to Thane, 34 km.
  • First electric train: 1925, Bombay VT to Kurla.
  • Railways were nationalised in 1951. Railway Board headquarters: Rail Bhawan, New Delhi.
  • First Rajdhani Express: 1969 (New Delhi-Howrah). First Shatabdi Express: 1988 (New Delhi-Bhopal).
  • Vande Bharat Express launched in February 2019 (New Delhi-Varanasi).
  • Konkan Railway started operations in 1998.
  • National Rail Museum is in Chanakyapuri, New Delhi (opened 1977).
  • Chenab Rail Bridge (Jammu & Kashmir) is the world's highest railway arch bridge.

6. Static GK & National Symbols

  • National Animal: Tiger. Bird: Peacock. Flower: Lotus. Fruit: Mango. Tree: Banyan. River: Ganga.
  • National Aquatic Animal: Ganges River Dolphin. National Heritage Animal: Elephant.
  • National Anthem: Jana Gana Mana (adopted 24 January 1950). National Song: Vande Mataram.
  • Highest civilian award: Bharat Ratna. Highest gallantry award: Param Vir Chakra.
  • Highest sports honour: Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna (renamed in 2021).
  • National Sport: none officially declared (hockey is commonly mistaken for it).
