LCM and HCF

RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 03

About this chapter

Factors, multiples, prime factorisation, HCF, LCM and word problems. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • A factor divides a number exactly.
  • A multiple is obtained by multiplying a number by an integer.
  • HCF is the greatest common factor shared by the given numbers.
  • LCM is the smallest positive common multiple.
  • For two positive integers: product = HCF × LCM.

Important formulas and reminders

Solved examples

Practice concept 1

Find the HCF of 18 and 24.

Answer: 6

18 = 2 × 3² and 24 = 2³ × 3. Common prime factors with the lowest powers give 2 × 3 = 6.

Practice concept 2

Find the LCM of 6 and 8.

Answer: 24

6 = 2 × 3 and 8 = 2³. Take the highest powers: 2³ × 3 = 24.

Practice concept 3

The product of two numbers is 180 and their HCF is 3. Find their LCM.

Answer: 60

LCM = product ÷ HCF = 180 ÷ 3 = 60.
