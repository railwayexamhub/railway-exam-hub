Calendar and Clock

RRB Group D Mathematics • Chapter 20

About this chapter

Calendar questions use leap-year rules and odd days. Clock questions use the movement of the hour and minute hands.

Study tip: Read the rules, practise the worked examples, then attempt the quiz without looking at the notes.

1. Key concepts and rules

  • A normal year has 365 days; a leap year has 366 days.
  • A leap year is divisible by 4, except century years must be divisible by 400.
  • A week has 7 days; odd days are the remainder after complete weeks.
  • The minute hand moves 6° per minute.
  • The hour hand moves 0.5° per minute.

2. Important formulas and reminders

Odd days = Total days modulo 7
Minute hand angle = 6° × minutes
Hour hand angle = 0.5° × minutes
Angle between hands = |30H − 5.5M|, then choose the smaller angle if required

3. Solved examples

Example 1: Is 2024 a leap year?

2024 is divisible by 4 and is not a century year.

Answer: Yes

Example 2: How many odd days are in 100 days?

100 ÷ 7 leaves remainder 2.

Answer: 2 odd days

Example 3: Find the angle moved by the minute hand in 15 minutes.

6×15.

Answer: 90°

4. Exam tips

  • Write down the given values before selecting a formula.
  • Check that units are consistent.
  • Do calculations step by step and simplify the final answer.
  • Estimate the result to catch arithmetic mistakes.
  • Practise questions under a time limit after learning the method.
