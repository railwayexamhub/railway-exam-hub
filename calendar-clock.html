Calendar and Clock

RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 20

About this chapter

Leap years, odd days, day calculations and angles between clock hands. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • A leap year has 366 days; an ordinary year has 365 days.
  • A leap year is divisible by 4, except century years must be divisible by 400.
  • A week has 7 days.
  • Minute hand moves 6° per minute; hour hand moves 0.5° per minute.
  • Angle between hands = absolute difference of their positions; use the smaller angle.

Solved examples

Practice concept 1

How many days are there in a leap year?

Answer: 366

A leap year has 366 days.

Practice concept 2

How many degrees does the minute hand move in 10 minutes?

Answer: 60°

The minute hand moves 6° per minute. In 10 minutes: 6 × 10 = 60°.

Practice concept 3

Which of these is a leap year?

Answer: 2024

2024 is divisible by 4 and is not a century year.
