About the Examination

RRB Group D, also known as Level 1 recruitment, is conducted by Railway Recruitment Boards for various railway posts. Check the relevant official notification for vacancies, eligibility, selection stages and exam pattern.

Subjects and Syllabus

🔢 Mathematics

Number System, BODMAS, LCM, HCF, Fractions, Percentage, Ratio, Profit and Loss, Interest, Time and Work, Speed and Distance, Mensuration and more.

🧠 General Intelligence & Reasoning

Analogy, Series, Coding-Decoding, Classification, Directions, Relationships, Venn Diagrams and logical reasoning.

🔬 General Science

Physics, Chemistry and Life Science topics generally based on school-level concepts. Check the official notification.

🌍 General Awareness

Current affairs, Indian history, geography, polity, economy, sports, culture and important events.
