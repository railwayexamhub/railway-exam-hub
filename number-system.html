Chapter 1: Number System

Learn the basic concepts of numbers, divisibility, factors, multiples, HCF, LCM and remainders.

Study method: Read the concepts, solve the examples yourself, attempt the practice questions and then check the explanations.

1. Types of Numbers

A number system classifies numbers according to their properties.

TypeMeaningExamples
NaturalCounting numbers starting from 11, 2, 3, ...

Remember: 1 is neither prime nor composite. 2 is the only even prime number.

2. Factors and Multiples

Factor: A number that divides another number exactly.

Example: Factors of 12 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 12.

Multiple: A number obtained by multiplying a number by an integer.

Example: Multiples of 5 are 5, 10, 15, 20, ...

Prime factorisation: Expressing a number as a product of prime numbers.

Example: 60 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 5 = 2² × 3 × 5.

4. HCF and LCM

HCF: The greatest number that divides all the given numbers exactly.

LCM: The smallest positive number that is a multiple of all the given numbers.

Worked example

12 = 2² × 3

18 = 2 × 3²

HCF = 2 × 3 = 6 (take common prime factors with smaller powers).

LCM = 2² × 3² = 36 (take all prime factors with greater powers).

Useful relation: First number × Second number = HCF × LCM.

5. Remainders

When a number a is divided by a positive divisor d:

a = d × q + r

Here, q is the quotient and r is the remainder.

The remainder must satisfy: 0 ≤ r < d.

Example: 29 ÷ 6 gives quotient 4 and remainder 5, because 29 = 6 × 4 + 5.

6. Worked Examples

Example 1: Is 7,452 divisible by 3?

7 + 4 + 5 + 2 = 18. Since 18 is divisible by 3, 7,452 is divisible by 3.

Example 2: Find the HCF of 24 and 36.

24 = 2³ × 3; 36 = 2² × 3².

HCF = 2² × 3 = 12.

Example 3: Find the LCM of 8, 12 and 18.

8 = 2³; 12 = 2² × 3; 18 = 2 × 3².

LCM = 2³ × 3² = 72.

Example 4: Find the remainder when 53 is divided by 7.

7 × 7 = 49; 53 − 49 = 4.

Remainder = 4.

Example 5: How many positive factors does 36 have?

36 = 2² × 3².

Number of factors = (2 + 1)(2 + 1) = 9.

9. Quick Revision

  • 1 is neither prime nor composite.
  • 2 is the only even prime number.
  • A prime number has exactly two positive factors.
  • For two positive integers: Product = HCF × LCM.
  • If N = pᵃ × qᵇ, number of positive factors = (a + 1)(b + 1).
  • Remainder is always smaller than the divisor.
  • For divisibility by 3 or 9, add the digits.
  • For divisibility by 4, check the last two digits.
