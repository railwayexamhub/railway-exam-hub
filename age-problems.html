Problems on Ages

RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 19

About this chapter

Present ages, age differences, past and future ages and equations. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • If present age is x, age after n years is x+n.
  • Age n years ago is x−n.
  • The age difference between two people remains constant.
  • Translate age statements into equations before solving.

Solved examples

Practice concept 1

A person's present age is 20. What will it be after 5 years?

Answer: 25 years

Future age = 20 + 5 = 25 years.

Practice concept 2

A father is 40 and his son is 10. What is their age difference?

Answer: 30 years

Difference = 40 − 10 = 30 years.

Practice concept 3

A person was 18 years old 4 years ago. Present age?

Answer: 22 years

Present age = 18 + 4 = 22 years.
