Trigonometry

RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 17

About this chapter

Trigonometric ratios in right triangles and standard values. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • sin θ = perpendicular/hypotenuse.
  • cos θ = base/hypotenuse.
  • tan θ = perpendicular/base = sin θ/cos θ.
  • sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.
  • tan 45° = 1; sin 30° = 1/2; cos 60° = 1/2.
