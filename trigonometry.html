Trigonometry

RRB Group D Mathematics • Chapter 17

About this chapter

Basic trigonometry relates the angles of a right-angled triangle to ratios of its sides.

Study tip: Read the rules, practise the worked examples, then attempt the quiz without looking at the notes.

1. Key concepts and rules

  • Identify the opposite, adjacent and hypotenuse sides relative to the chosen angle.
  • The hypotenuse is opposite the right angle and is the longest side.
  • Learn standard values for 0°, 30°, 45°, 60° and 90°.
  • Use sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.

2. Important formulas and reminders

sin θ = Opposite ÷ Hypotenuse
cos θ = Adjacent ÷ Hypotenuse
tan θ = Opposite ÷ Adjacent
tan θ = sin θ ÷ cos θ
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1

3. Solved examples

Example 1: In a right triangle, opposite = 3 and hypotenuse = 5. Find sin θ.

sin θ = 3/5.

Answer: 3/5

Example 2: Find tan 45°.

At 45°, opposite and adjacent sides are equal.

Answer: 1

Example 3: If sin θ = 3/5 for an acute angle, find cos θ.

Using a 3-4-5 triangle, adjacent = 4 and hypotenuse = 5.

Answer: 4/5

4. Exam tips

  • Write down the given values before selecting a formula.
  • Check that units are consistent.
  • Do calculations step by step and simplify the final answer.
  • Estimate the result to catch arithmetic mistakes.
  • Practise questions under a time limit after learning the method.
