About this preparation area

Station Master is a graduate-level post recruited through RRB NTPC. This page separates Station Master preparation from general NTPC preparation so that post-specific stages and aptitude preparation are not confused with other railway recruitments.

Recruitment stages to prepare for

  1. CBT 1: General Awareness, Mathematics and General Intelligence & Reasoning.
  2. CBT 2: General Awareness, Mathematics and General Intelligence & Reasoning at the applicable level.
  3. CBAT: Computer-Based Aptitude Test for posts for which it is prescribed, including Station Master. Prepare according to the current official test instructions.
  4. Document verification and medical examination: As specified in the applicable notification.

Important: The applicable notification governs selection stages, qualifying requirements, test structure, medical standards and eligibility. Always check the current RRB CEN and official CBAT instructions.

Study subjects

  • Mathematics: number system, arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, statistics and other notified topics.
  • General Intelligence & Reasoning: analytical and verbal/non-verbal reasoning topics in the notified syllabus.
  • General Awareness: current affairs, general knowledge, science and other areas listed in the notification.
  • CBAT preparation: understand the aptitude-test format, instructions and practice requirements from the official RRB material. Do not assume ordinary CBT MCQs substitute for CBAT practice.
