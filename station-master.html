RRB NTPC — Station Master

Graduate-level post · CEN 06/2025 reference

Selection stages

  1. 1st Stage CBT (common for NTPC graduate posts)
  2. 2nd Stage CBT (common for NTPC graduate posts)
  3. Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) for candidates who opt for Station Master / Traffic Assistant
  4. Document Verification and Medical Examination

Important: Station Master candidates must qualify in every CBAT test battery. The official CEN requires a minimum T-score of 42 in each battery; no category relaxation is stated for this threshold.

CBT syllabus

Mathematics

Number system, decimals, fractions, LCM, HCF, ratio and proportion, percentage, mensuration, time and work, time and distance, simple and compound interest, profit and loss, elementary algebra, geometry, trigonometry and elementary statistics.

General Intelligence & Reasoning

Analogies, number and alphabet series, coding-decoding, mathematical operations, relationships, syllogism, jumbling, Venn diagrams, data interpretation and sufficiency, conclusions and decision-making, similarities and differences, analytical reasoning, classification, directions, statement-argument and assumption questions.

General Awareness

Current events of national and international importance, games and sports, art and culture of India, Indian literature, monuments and places, general science and life science (up to 10th CBSE), history of India and freedom struggle, geography, polity and constitution, scientific and technological developments, space and nuclear programmes, UN and other world organisations, environment, basics of computers, abbreviations, transport systems, economy, famous personalities, flagship government programmes, flora and fauna, important government and public-sector organisations.

Station Master CBAT

  • CBAT applies to candidates who opt for Station Master / Traffic Assistant and qualify the 2nd Stage CBT.
  • Minimum T-score 42 is required in each test battery.
  • No negative marking in CBAT; questions and answer options are in English and Hindi.
  • Merit weightage for Station Master: 70% from 2nd Stage CBT and 30% from CBAT, among candidates who qualify CBAT.
  • Bring the prescribed original vision certificate as required by the notification.

Use the official RDSO Psycho-Technical Directorate aptitude-test guidance for CBAT preparation. Ordinary CBT practice alone is not a substitute for aptitude-test familiarisation.
