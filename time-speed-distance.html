Time, Speed and Distance

RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 12

About this chapter

Speed, distance, time, unit conversion and relative speed. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • Speed = Distance ÷ Time.
  • Distance = Speed × Time.
  • Time = Distance ÷ Speed.
  • 1 km/h = 5/18 m/s.
  • For opposite directions, relative speed is the sum; for same direction, it is the difference.

Solved examples

Practice concept 1

A vehicle travels 120 km in 2 hours. Find its speed.

Answer: 60 km/h

Speed = 120 ÷ 2 = 60 km/h.

Practice concept 2

Convert 36 km/h into m/s.

Answer: 10 m/s

36 × 5/18 = 10 m/s.

Practice concept 3

How far does a vehicle travel at 50 km/h in 3 hours?

Answer: 150 km

Distance = speed × time = 50 × 3 = 150 km.
