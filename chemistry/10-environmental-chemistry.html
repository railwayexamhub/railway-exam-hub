Environmental Chemistry

RRB Group D General Science · Chemistry Chapter 10

About this chapter

Environmental chemistry studies chemical processes in air, water and soil. Pollution can harm ecosystems and human health.

Study method: Read the concept summary, learn the key points, work through the examples, then attempt the quiz and review every explanation.

1. Core concepts and exam notes

  • Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit infrared radiation; increasing concentrations contribute to warming.
  • Sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides can contribute to acid deposition.
  • Ozone in the stratosphere absorbs harmful ultraviolet radiation; ground-level ozone is a pollutant.
  • Reduce, reuse, recycle, treat wastewater and prevent uncontrolled emissions.

2. Worked examples and applications

Worked example 1

A major source of carbon dioxide emissions is burning fossil fuels.
