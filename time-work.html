Time and Work

RRB Group D Mathematics • Chapter 10

About this chapter

Work-rate questions compare the amount of work completed per unit of time.

Study tip: Read the rules, practise the worked examples, then attempt the quiz without looking at the notes.

1. Key concepts and rules

  • If a person completes a job in x days, one-day work is 1/x.
  • Add individual daily work rates when people work together.
  • Time taken together is the reciprocal of the combined rate.
  • For equal efficiency, time is inversely proportional to number of workers.

2. Important formulas and reminders

One-day work = 1 ÷ days required
Combined rate = 1/a + 1/b
Combined time = 1 ÷ combined rate
Work = Rate × Time

3. Solved examples

Example 1: A completes a job in 10 days. What is A's one-day work?

One-day work = 1/10.

Answer: 1/10 of the job

Example 2: A takes 6 days and B takes 3 days. How long together?

Rate = 1/6+1/3 = 1/2 job per day.

Answer: 2 days

Example 3: 12 workers finish a job in 10 days. How many worker-days are needed?

Worker-days = 12×10.

Answer: 120 worker-days

4. Exam tips

  • Write down the given values before selecting a formula.
  • Check that units are consistent.
  • Do calculations step by step and simplify the final answer.
  • Estimate the result to catch arithmetic mistakes.
  • Practise questions under a time limit after learning the method.
