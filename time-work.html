Time and Work

RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 10

About this chapter

Work rates, combined work, efficiency and work completed in given time. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • If a person completes a job in x days, one-day work = 1/x.
  • Combined rate = sum of individual rates when working together.
  • Time = Total work ÷ Work rate.
  • Efficiency is inversely proportional to time for the same work.

Important formulas and reminders

Solved examples

Practice concept 1

A completes a job in 10 days. What fraction is completed in one day?

Answer: 1/10

One-day work is the reciprocal of the total days: 1/10.

Practice concept 2

A can finish in 6 days and B in 3 days. Together, how many days?

Answer: 2 days

Combined rate = 1/6 + 1/3 = 1/2 job per day. Time = 2 days.

Practice concept 3

If 4 workers finish a job in 6 days, how many worker-days are needed?

Answer: 24

Worker-days = 4 × 6 = 24.
