RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 14

About this chapter

Perimeter, area and volume of common two-dimensional and three-dimensional shapes. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • Rectangle area = length × breadth; perimeter = 2(l+b).
  • Square area = side²; perimeter = 4 × side.
  • Triangle area = 1/2 × base × height.
  • Circle area = πr²; circumference = 2πr.
  • Cuboid volume = l × b × h; cube volume = side³.

Solved examples

Practice concept 1

Find the area of a rectangle of length 8 cm and breadth 5 cm.

Answer: 40 cm²

Area = 8 × 5 = 40 cm².

Practice concept 2

Find the circumference of a circle of radius 7 cm. Use π = 22/7.

Answer: 44 cm

Circumference = 2 × 22/7 × 7 = 44 cm.

Practice concept 3

Volume of a cube of side 3 cm is:

Answer: 27 cm³

Volume = side³ = 3³ = 27 cm³.
