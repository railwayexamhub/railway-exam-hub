Mensuration

RRB Group D Mathematics • Chapter 14

About this chapter

Mensuration measures perimeter, area, surface area and volume of two-dimensional and three-dimensional shapes.

Study tip: Read the rules, practise the worked examples, then attempt the quiz without looking at the notes.

1. Key concepts and rules

  • Perimeter is the total boundary length.
  • Area is the surface enclosed by a 2D shape.
  • Volume measures the space occupied by a 3D solid.
  • Use square units for area and cubic units for volume.
  • Keep all dimensions in the same unit.

2. Important formulas and reminders

Rectangle: Area = l×b; Perimeter = 2(l+b)
Square: Area = a²; Perimeter = 4a
Triangle: Area = 1/2×base×height
Circle: Area = πr²; Circumference = 2πr
Cuboid: Volume = lbh
Cube: Volume = a³
Cylinder: Volume = πr²h

3. Solved examples

Example 1: Find the area of a rectangle 12 cm by 5 cm.

Area = 12×5.

Answer: 60 cm²

Example 2: Find the circumference of a circle of radius 7 cm. Use π=22/7.

2×22/7×7.

Answer: 44 cm

Example 3: Find the volume of a cube with side 4 cm.

Volume = 4³.

Answer: 64 cm³

4. Exam tips

  • Write down the given values before selecting a formula.
  • Check that units are consistent.
  • Do calculations step by step and simplify the final answer.
  • Estimate the result to catch arithmetic mistakes.
  • Practise questions under a time limit after learning the method.
