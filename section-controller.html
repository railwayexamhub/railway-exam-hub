Selection stages

  1. Single-stage Computer Based Test (CBT)
  2. Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) for candidates shortlisted from CBT
  3. Document Verification (DV)
  4. Medical Examination

CBT shortlisting for CBAT is normally up to eight times the vacancies. Final merit among CBAT-qualified candidates uses 70% CBT and 30% CBAT weightage.

Detailed CBT syllabus

1. Analytical and Mathematical Capability

Mathematics:

  • Number System
  • Ratio and Proportions
  • Averages
  • Percentages
  • Profit, Loss and Discounts
  • Time, Speed and Distance
  • Power and Work
  • Algebra and Linear Equations
  • Arithmetic Progression
  • LCM and HCF
  • Geometry
  • Area and Volumes
  • Probability and Statistics (basic level)

Data Analysis and Interpretation:

  • Analysis and inference from text and multi-source data
  • Tabular and graphical data
  • Charts, graphs, scatter plots and pie charts
  • Statistical curve distributions and Venn diagrams
  • Data sufficiency and data arrangement

2. Logical Capability

  • Binary Logic
  • Syllogism
  • Clocks and Calendars
  • Assumptions
  • Blood Relations and Family Tree
  • Logic-based puzzles
  • Reading comprehension: main idea, supporting idea, application, logical structure and writing style

3. Mental Reasoning

  • Analogy and relationship identification
  • Series completion and number patterns
  • Coding-Decoding
  • Ranking and arrangement problems

The official notification describes the topics as illustrative and not necessarily exhaustive.

CBAT: important rules

  • Shortlisted candidates must qualify in each test battery.
  • Minimum qualifying T-score: 42 in each test battery, with no category relaxation.
  • No negative marking in CBAT.
  • CBAT questions and answer options are in English and Hindi.
  • Bring the prescribed original vision certificate as specified in the notification.
  • Final merit uses 70% CBT marks and 30% CBAT marks, among candidates who qualify CBAT.

Preparation plan

  1. Build accuracy in arithmetic, algebra, geometry and basic statistics.
  2. Practise data interpretation using tables, graphs, pie charts and mixed information.
  3. Develop logical reasoning through syllogisms, binary logic, puzzles and family-tree questions.
  4. Practise reading-comprehension passages from varied topics and identify the main idea and logical structure.
  5. Prepare separately for aptitude-test batteries using the official RDSO guidance; ordinary CBT practice is not a substitute for CBAT familiarisation.
  6. Take timed 100-question practice tests and review every incorrect answer.
