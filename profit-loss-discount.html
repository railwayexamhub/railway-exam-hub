Profit, Loss and Discount

RRB Group D Mathematics • Chapter 7

About this chapter

Profit and loss compare selling price with cost price. Discount is calculated on marked price.

Study tip: Read the rules, practise the worked examples, then attempt the quiz without looking at the notes.

1. Key concepts and rules

  • Profit occurs when selling price (SP) is greater than cost price (CP).
  • Loss occurs when SP is less than CP.
  • Profit% and loss% are normally calculated on CP.
  • Discount is the reduction from marked price (MP), calculated on MP.

2. Important formulas and reminders

Profit = SP − CP
Loss = CP − SP
Profit% = (Profit ÷ CP) × 100
Loss% = (Loss ÷ CP) × 100
Discount% = (MP − SP) ÷ MP × 100

3. Solved examples

Example 1: An article costs ₹400 and sells for ₹460. Find profit percentage.

Profit = 460−400 = ₹60. Profit% = 60/400 × 100.

Answer: 15%

Example 2: An article marked ₹800 is sold at 10% discount.

Discount = ₹80; SP = 800−80.

Answer: ₹720

Example 3: An article is sold for ₹540 at a loss of 10%. Find CP.

SP is 90% of CP. CP = 540/0.9.

Answer: ₹600

4. Exam tips

  • Write down the given values before selecting a formula.
  • Check that units are consistent.
  • Do calculations step by step and simplify the final answer.
  • Estimate the result to catch arithmetic mistakes.
  • Practise questions under a time limit after learning the method.
