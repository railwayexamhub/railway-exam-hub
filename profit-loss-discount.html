Profit, Loss and Discount

RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 07

About this chapter

Cost price, selling price, profit, loss, marked price and discount calculations. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • Profit = Selling Price − Cost Price.
  • Loss = Cost Price − Selling Price.
  • Profit% = (Profit ÷ Cost Price) × 100.
  • Loss% = (Loss ÷ Cost Price) × 100.
  • Discount = Marked Price − Selling Price; Discount% is calculated on Marked Price.

Important formulas and reminders

Solved examples

Practice concept 1

An article costs ₹400 and sells for ₹460. Find the profit.

Answer: ₹60

Profit = 460 − 400 = ₹60.

Practice concept 2

Cost price is ₹500 and loss is 10%. Find selling price.

Answer: ₹450

Loss = 10% of 500 = ₹50. Selling price = 500 − 50 = ₹450.

Practice concept 3

Marked price is ₹800 with a 15% discount. Find selling price.

Answer: ₹680

Discount = 15% of 800 = ₹120. Selling price = 800 − 120 = ₹680.
