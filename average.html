Average

RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 09

About this chapter

Arithmetic mean, combined average and missing-value questions. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • Average = Sum of observations ÷ Number of observations.
  • Sum = Average × Number of observations.
  • When one value is added, update the total before finding the new average.
  • For equally spaced consecutive numbers, average is the middle value.

Important formulas and reminders

Solved examples

Practice concept 1

Find the average of 10, 20 and 30.

Answer: 20

Average = (10 + 20 + 30)/3 = 60/3 = 20.

Practice concept 2

The average of 5 numbers is 12. Find their sum.

Answer: 60

Sum = average × count = 12 × 5 = 60.

Practice concept 3

Average of the first five natural numbers is:

Answer: 3

The numbers are 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. Their sum is 15; 15/5 = 3.
