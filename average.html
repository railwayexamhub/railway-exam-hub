Average

RRB Group D Mathematics • Chapter 9

About this chapter

The average is the equal share obtained when a total is distributed equally among a number of items.

Study tip: Read the rules, practise the worked examples, then attempt the quiz without looking at the notes.

1. Key concepts and rules

  • Average is also called arithmetic mean.
  • If average and count are known, total = average × count.
  • When one value is replaced, compare the change in total.
  • For equally spaced values, the average is the middle value when there are an odd number of terms.

2. Important formulas and reminders

Average = Sum of observations ÷ Number of observations
Sum = Average × Number of observations
Combined average = Combined total ÷ Combined count

3. Solved examples

Example 1: Find the average of 10, 20 and 30.

Sum = 60; count = 3; average = 60/3.

Answer: 20

Example 2: The average of 5 numbers is 18. Find their sum.

Sum = 18×5.

Answer: 90

Example 3: The average of 4 numbers is 15. A fifth number 20 is added. Find the new average.

Old sum = 60. New sum = 80; count = 5.

Answer: 16

4. Exam tips

  • Write down the given values before selecting a formula.
  • Check that units are consistent.
  • Do calculations step by step and simplify the final answer.
  • Estimate the result to catch arithmetic mistakes.
  • Practise questions under a time limit after learning the method.
