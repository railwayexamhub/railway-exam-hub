Work, Energy and Power

RRB Group D General Science · Physics Chapter 04

About this chapter

In physics, work is done when a force causes displacement. For a constant force parallel to displacement, W = Fs. Energy is the capacity to do work. Kinetic energy is KE = ½mv²; gravitational potential energy near Earth's surface is PE = mgh. Power is the rate of doing work: P = W/t.

Study method: Read the concept summary, learn the key points, work through the examples, then attempt the quiz and review every explanation.

1. Core concepts and exam notes

  • SI unit of work and energy is joule (J); 1 J = 1 N m.
  • SI unit of power is watt (W); 1 W = 1 J/s.
  • Work can be positive, negative or zero depending on the angle between force and displacement.
  • The law of conservation of energy states that energy is transformed, not created or destroyed in an isolated system.

2. Worked examples and applications

Worked example 1

A 10 N force moves an object 3 m in its direction. Work = 10 × 3 = 30 J.

Worked example 2

A 100 J task completed in 5 s requires average power 100/5 = 20 W.
