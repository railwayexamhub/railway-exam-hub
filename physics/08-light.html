Light

RRB Group D General Science · Physics · Chapter 08

About this chapter

Light is electromagnetic radiation visible to the human eye; it can travel through vacuum.

Study method: Understand each concept, learn the important relations and units, work through the examples, then attempt all 25 questions and review the explanations.

1. Complete concept notes

  1. Light travels in straight lines in a uniform transparent medium. Its speed in vacuum is approximately 3 × 10⁸ m/s.
  2. Reflection is the bouncing back of light. The angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection, measured from the normal.
  3. A plane mirror forms a virtual, erect image of the same size, laterally inverted and as far behind the mirror as the object is in front.
  4. Refraction is bending of light when its speed changes as it enters another medium. It is described by Snell's law.
  5. A convex lens converges parallel rays; a concave lens diverges them. Lens power is measured in dioptres.
  6. A convex mirror gives a virtual, erect and diminished image and provides a wide field of view, useful in vehicle mirrors.
  7. Dispersion separates white light into colours because different wavelengths refract by different amounts.
  8. The human eye focuses light on the retina. The iris controls pupil size; the lens changes shape for accommodation.

2. Important formulas and facts

  • n = c/v
  • P = 1/f (f in metres)
  • 1/f = 1/v − 1/u (Cartesian sign convention)

3. Worked examples

A lens has focal length 0.5 m. Find its power.

Answer: 2 D

P = 1/f = 1/0.5 = 2 dioptres.
