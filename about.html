About Railway Exam Hub

Railway Exam Hub is a free preparation site for students aiming for jobs in Indian Railways. It brings together exam pages, topic-wise practice questions and mock tests for RRB Group D, NTPC, ALP, Technician, JE, RPF and other recruitments.

What you will find here

  • Exam pages that explain each recruitment and what to study
  • Maths and science practice questions with answers
  • Timed mock tests to check your preparation
