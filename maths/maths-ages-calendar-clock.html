Maths: Ages, Calendar & Clock

RRB Group D / NTPC / ALP / JE • Concept, tricks, solved examples, practice

1. Formulas & concepts

  • Ages: take present age as x; use x ± years for past and future; the age difference never changes
  • Clock: angle = |30H − 5.5M|; hands coincide 11 times in 12 hours (22 in a day), at right angles 22 times a day
  • Odd days: ordinary year 1, leap year 2; 100 years = 5, 200 = 3, 300 = 1, 400 = 0
  • Leap year: divisible by 4; century years only if divisible by 400
  • Odd days by month: 31-day months = 3, 30-day months = 2, February = 0 (1 in leap year)

2. Memory tricks

  • Day code: Sun 0, Mon 1, Tue 2, Wed 3, Thu 4, Fri 5, Sat 6. Total odd days ÷ 7, then use the remainder.
  • "31 = 3, 30 = 2" for odd days in each month.
  • Clock speeds: minute hand 6°/min, hour hand 0.5°/min, relative 5.5°/min.
  • Age difference stays fixed, so build equations around the difference.

3. Solved examples

Ex 1. Present ages sum to 50 in the ratio 2:3. Find them.
Ratio sum = 5, so one part = 10. Ages 20 and 30.
Ex 2. Angle between the hands at 4:30.
|30 × 4 − 5.5 × 30| = |120 − 165| = 45°.
Ex 3. 1 January 2001 was Monday. Day on 1 January 2002?
2001 is an ordinary year = 1 odd day. Monday + 1 = Tuesday.

4. Practice (tap to see answer)

Q1. Father is 40, son is 10. After how many years will the father be twice the son's age?

(a) 10 (b) 15 (c) 20 (d) 25

Answer: (c) 20 (40 + x = 2(10 + x))

Q2. Angle between the hands at 6:00?

(a) 90° (b) 120° (c) 150° (d) 180°

Answer: (d) 180°

Q3. Which is a leap year?

(a) 1900 (b) 2000 (c) 2100 (d) 2022

Answer: (b) 2000

Q4. Today is Wednesday. What day will it be after 100 days?

(a) Thursday (b) Friday (c) Saturday (d) Sunday

Answer: (b) Friday (100 ÷ 7 leaves 2)

Q5. How many times do the hands coincide in 12 hours?

(a) 10 (b) 11 (c) 12 (d) 22

Answer: (b) 11

5. Expanded lesson: Age equations and clock movement

Represent present ages with variables before applying the past or future condition. On a clock, the minute hand moves 6° per minute and the hour hand moves 0.5° per minute.
Additional solved example 1.
A father is 4 times his son's age. Their total is 50. Find their ages.
Solution: Let son's age=x. 5x=50, so son=10 and father=40.
Additional solved example 2.
Find the smaller angle at 2:30.
Solution: |30×2 − 5.5×30| = 105°.
