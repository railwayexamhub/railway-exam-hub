Maths: Simple & Compound Interest

RRB Group D / NTPC / ALP / JE • Concept, tricks, solved examples, practice

1. Formulas & concepts

  • SI = P × R × T / 100; Amount = P + SI
  • CI: A = P(1 + R/100)T; CI = A − P
  • Half-yearly: rate R/2, time 2T; quarterly: R/4, 4T
  • For 2 years: CI − SI = P(R/100)²
  • 2-year effective rate = R + R + R²/100
  • SI doubles money in 100/R years; CI roughly in 72/R years

2. Memory tricks

  • PRT/100: say it as "Pee-Are-Tee over hundred".
  • Rule of 72: at 8% compound, money doubles in 72/8 = 9 years (approximate).
  • Year 1: SI = CI. From year 2, CI is larger because interest earns interest.
  • 2-year CI shortcut: 10% for 2 years = 10 + 10 + 1 = 21% total.
  • Half-yearly: halve the rate, double the time.

3. Solved examples

Ex 1. SI on ₹5000 at 8% for 3 years.
5000 × 8 × 3/100 = ₹1200.
Ex 2. CI on ₹10000 at 10% for 2 years.
Effective 21%. CI = ₹2100.
Ex 3. CI − SI for 2 years at 10% is ₹20. Find P.
P × (10/100)² = 20, so P = ₹2000.

4. Practice (tap to see answer)

Q1. SI on ₹2400 at 5% for 2 years?

(a) 200 (b) 220 (c) 240 (d) 260

Answer: (c) ₹240

Q2. Amount on ₹8000 at 5% CI for 2 years?

(a) 8800 (b) 8820 (c) 8840 (d) 8900

Answer: (b) ₹8820

Q3. At SI, money doubles in 5 years. Rate?

(a) 10% (b) 15% (c) 20% (d) 25%

Answer: (c) 20%

Q4. ₹10000 at 20% p.a. compounded half-yearly for 1 year. CI?

(a) 2000 (b) 2100 (c) 2200 (d) 2400

Answer: (b) ₹2100 (10% twice = 21%)

Q5. CI − SI for 2 years on ₹5000 at 4%?

(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 10 (d) 12

Answer: (b) ₹8 (5000 × 0.0016)

5. Expanded lesson: Compounding periods and growth

For annual compounding, A=P(1+R/100)^T. For half-yearly compounding, use R/2 for each of 2T periods. For depreciation, multiply by (1−R/100) for each period.
Additional solved example 1.
Find CI on ₹2,000 at 10% for 2 years.
Solution: Amount=2000×1.1²=₹2,420; CI=₹420.
Additional solved example 2.
Find SI on ₹3,000 at 8% for 9 months.
Solution: SI=3000×8×(9/12)/100=₹180.
