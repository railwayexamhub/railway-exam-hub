Maths: LCM & HCF

RRB Group D / NTPC / ALP / JE • Concept, tricks, solved examples, practice

1. Formulas & concepts

  • Product of two numbers = LCM × HCF
  • HCF of fractions = HCF of numerators / LCM of denominators; LCM of fractions = LCM of numerators / HCF of denominators
  • Largest number dividing a, b, c leaving remainders r₁, r₂, r₃ = HCF(a−r₁, b−r₂, c−r₃)
  • Smallest number leaving the same remainder r = LCM + r
  • Bells ringing together, runners meeting again, tiles to cover a floor: LCM / HCF

2. Memory tricks

  • HCF ≤ smallest number; LCM ≥ largest number.
  • "H divides, L multiplies": HCF = greatest divisor, LCM = least multiple.
  • "Subtract for HCF, add for LCM": remainders are subtracted first for HCF, added at the end for LCM.
  • Prime method: HCF takes the lowest powers of common primes; LCM takes the highest powers of all primes.
  • Tiles/largest square: side = HCF of length and breadth.

3. Solved examples

Ex 1. HCF = 12, LCM = 180, one number = 36. Find the other.
12 × 180 / 36 = 60.
Ex 2. LCM of 12, 18, 30?
12 = 2²×3, 18 = 2×3², 30 = 2×3×5. LCM = 4 × 9 × 5 = 180.
Ex 3. Largest number dividing 245 and 1029 leaving remainder 5 each time?
HCF(240, 1024) = 16.

4. Practice (tap to see answer)

Q1. HCF of 24 and 36?

(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 12 (d) 24

Answer: (c) 12

Q2. LCM of 8 and 12?

(a) 16 (b) 24 (c) 36 (d) 48

Answer: (b) 24

Q3. Smallest number leaving remainder 3 when divided by 6, 8 and 12?

(a) 24 (b) 27 (c) 30 (d) 51

Answer: (b) 27 (LCM 24 + 3)

Q4. Product of two numbers 2160, HCF 12. LCM = ?

(a) 144 (b) 180 (c) 216 (d) 240

Answer: (b) 180

Q5. Bells ring every 12, 15 and 20 seconds. Together again after?

(a) 30 s (b) 45 s (c) 60 s (d) 90 s

Answer: (c) 60 s

5. Expanded lesson: Choosing HCF or LCM

Use HCF when the question asks for the greatest equal size, maximum number of identical groups, or largest measure that divides all quantities. Use LCM when events repeat and must coincide, or when finding the smallest common multiple.
Additional solved example 1.
Find HCF and LCM of 18 and 30.
Solution: HCF = 6; LCM = (18 × 30) ÷ 6 = 90.
Additional solved example 2.
Three bells ring every 8, 12 and 18 minutes. When together again?
Solution: LCM(8,12,18) = 72 minutes.
