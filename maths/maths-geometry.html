Maths: Geometry

RRB Group D / NTPC / ALP / JE • Concept, tricks, solved examples, practice

1. Formulas & concepts

  • Triangle angle sum = 180°; exterior angle = sum of the two opposite interior angles
  • Polygon: interior sum = (n−2)×180°; exterior sum = 360°; each interior (regular) = (n−2)×180/n; diagonals = n(n−3)/2
  • Pythagoras: a² + b² = c²; triples 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25
  • Circle: angle at centre = 2 × angle at circumference; angle in a semicircle = 90°; tangent ⊥ radius; cyclic quadrilateral: opposite angles sum to 180°
  • Centroid divides each median 2:1

2. Memory tricks

  • Four centres: Medians → Centroid; Altitudes → Orthocentre; Angle bisectors → Incentre; Perpendicular bisectors → Circumcentre.
  • Exterior angle of a regular polygon = 360/n. Interior 144° → exterior 36° → n = 10.
  • Scaled triples: 6-8-10, 9-12-15, 10-24-26.
  • Isosceles triangle: base angles are equal.

3. Solved examples

Ex 1. Angles of a triangle in ratio 2:3:4. Find them.
Sum 9 parts = 180°, 1 part = 20°. Angles 40°, 60°, 80°.
Ex 2. Each interior angle of a regular hexagon?
(6−2)×180/6 = 120°.
Ex 3. Right triangle with legs 9 and 12. Hypotenuse?
√(81 + 144) = √225 = 15.

4. Practice (tap to see answer)

Q1. Sum of interior angles of an octagon?

(a) 900° (b) 1080° (c) 1260° (d) 1440°

Answer: (b) 1080°

Q2. Angle at the centre is 80°. Angle at the circumference on the same arc?

(a) 20° (b) 40° (c) 80° (d) 160°

Answer: (b) 40°

Q3. Number of diagonals in a decagon?

(a) 20 (b) 30 (c) 35 (d) 45

Answer: (c) 35

Q4. Hypotenuse 13, one side 5. Other side?

(a) 8 (b) 10 (c) 12 (d) 14

Answer: (c) 12

Q5. Exterior angle of a regular polygon is 45°. Number of sides?

(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 9 (d) 10

Answer: (b) 8

5. Expanded lesson: Polygon angle rules

Interior angle sum of an n-sided polygon is (n−2)×180°. For a regular polygon, each exterior angle is 360°/n and each interior angle is 180°−360°/n.
Additional solved example 1.
Find each interior angle of a regular octagon.
Solution: Exterior angle = 360°/8 = 45°. Interior angle = 135°.
Additional solved example 2.
Find the interior angle sum of a hexagon.
Solution: (6−2)×180° = 720°.
