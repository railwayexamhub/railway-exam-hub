Maths: Trigonometry

RRB Group D / NTPC / ALP / JE • Concept, tricks, solved examples, practice

1. Formulas & concepts

  • sinθ = P/H, cosθ = B/H, tanθ = P/B (P perpendicular, B base, H hypotenuse)
  • sin²θ + cos²θ = 1; 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ; 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ
  • sin(90° − θ) = cosθ; tan(90° − θ) = cotθ
  • Height and distance: tanθ = height / distance
  • Angle of elevation (looking up) = angle of depression (looking down) from the other end

2. Memory tricks

  • Table: sin 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90° = √0/2, √1/2, √2/2, √3/2, √4/2 → 0, ½, 1/√2, √3/2, 1. cos is sin reversed. tan = sin ÷ cos.
  • "Pandit Badri Prasad Har Har Bole, Sona Chandi Tole" → sin = P/H, cos = B/H, tan = P/B.
  • Identities: divide sin² + cos² = 1 by cos² or sin² to get the other two.
  • tan 45° = 1, so a 45° elevation means height = distance.

3. Solved examples

Ex 1. sin 30° + cos 60° = ?
½ + ½ = 1.
Ex 2. sinθ = 3/5. Find cosθ.
cosθ = √(1 − 9/25) = 4/5.
Ex 3. Elevation of a tower top from a point 30 m away is 45°. Height?
tan 45° = 1, so height = 30 m.

4. Practice (tap to see answer)

Q1. sin²30° + cos²30° = ?

(a) 0 (b) 1/2 (c) 1 (d) 2

Answer: (c) 1

Q2. tan 45° × sin 90° = ?

(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) √3

Answer: (b) 1

Q3. cos 60° = ?

(a) 0 (b) 1/2 (c) √3/2 (d) 1

Answer: (b) 1/2

Q4. sinθ = 5/13. cosθ = ?

(a) 5/12 (b) 12/13 (c) 8/13 (d) 13/5

Answer: (b) 12/13

Q5. Elevation 30° from a point 60 m from the base. Height?

(a) 20√3 m (b) 30 m (c) 60√3 m (d) 60 m

Answer: (a) 20√3 m (60/√3)

5. Expanded lesson: Using trigonometry in right triangles

First mark the reference angle. Identify the opposite side, adjacent side and hypotenuse relative to that angle. Use SOH-CAH-TOA, and use Pythagoras if one side is missing.
Additional solved example 1.
If opposite=5 and adjacent=12, find tan θ and sin θ.
Solution: Hypotenuse=13. tan θ=5/12 and sin θ=5/13.
Additional solved example 2.
Evaluate 2 sin 30° + cos 0°.
Solution: 2×½ + 1 = 2.
