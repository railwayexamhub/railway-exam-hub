Maths: Average & Statistics

RRB Group D / NTPC / ALP / JE • Concept, tricks, solved examples, practice

1. Formulas & concepts

  • Average = Sum ÷ Number of items
  • Average of first n natural numbers = (n+1)/2; first n odd = n; first n even = n+1
  • Mean of an AP = (first + last)/2
  • Mode = 3 × Median − 2 × Mean
  • New member joins: new member = new total − old total
  • Weighted average = Σ(weight × value)/Σweights

2. Memory tricks

  • "Mode three-med, minus two-mean" = Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean.
  • Replacement trick: a person is replaced and the average rises by 2 in a group of 10 → the new person is 20 more.
  • Median: sort first; odd count = middle value; even count = average of the two middle values.
  • Consecutive numbers: the average is the middle number.

3. Solved examples

Ex 1. Average of 12, 18, 24, 30?
Sum 84 ÷ 4 = 21.
Ex 2. Average age of 5 people is 40. A 6th joins and the average becomes 42. Age of the new member?
6 × 42 − 5 × 40 = 252 − 200 = 52.
Ex 3. Mean = 20, Median = 18. Find the mode.
3 × 18 − 2 × 20 = 14.

4. Practice (tap to see answer)

Q1. Average of first 10 natural numbers?

(a) 5 (b) 5.5 (c) 6 (d) 10

Answer: (b) 5.5

Q2. Average of 5 numbers is 30. One number, 20, is removed. Average of the rest?

(a) 30 (b) 31 (c) 32.5 (d) 35

Answer: (c) 32.5

Q3. Mean 15, median 14. Mode = ?

(a) 12 (b) 13 (c) 14 (d) 16

Answer: (a) 12

Q4. Median of 3, 9, 5, 7, 1?

(a) 3 (b) 5 (c) 7 (d) 9

Answer: (b) 5 (sorted: 1,3,5,7,9)

Q5. Average of 7 consecutive numbers is 20. Largest number?

(a) 22 (b) 23 (c) 24 (d) 26

Answer: (b) 23

5. Expanded lesson: Average and replacement questions

Total = average × count. When one observation is replaced, new total = old total − removed value + added value. For combined groups, add their totals and divide by the combined count.
Additional solved example 1.
Average of 8 values is 15. One value 11 is replaced by 19. New average?
Solution: Old total=120; new total=128; new average=16.
Additional solved example 2.
Group of 20 has average 40 and group of 30 has average 50. Combined average?
Solution: (20×40 + 30×50)/50 = 46.
