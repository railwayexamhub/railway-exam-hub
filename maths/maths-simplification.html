Maths: Simplification, Fractions & Decimals

RRB Group D / NTPC / ALP / JE • Concept, tricks, solved examples, practice

1. Formulas & concepts

  • BODMAS: Brackets → Of → Division → Multiplication → Addition → Subtraction
  • (a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b²; a² − b² = (a+b)(a−b)
  • Recurring decimals: 0.ā = a/9; 0.ab = ab/99
  • Compare a/b and c/d by cross-multiplying: ad vs bc
  • Fraction × fraction: multiply tops and bottoms; division: flip and multiply

2. Memory tricks

  • D and M have equal rank, A and S too: go left to right within each pair.
  • 1 digit repeats → ÷9; 2 digits → ÷99; 3 digits → ÷999.
  • ×5: halve and ×10 (46×5 = 23×10 = 230). ×25: ÷4 then ×100. ×11: add neighbouring digits (35×11 → 3|3+5|5 = 385).
  • Difference of squares: 25² − 15² = (25+15)(25−15) = 400.

3. Solved examples

Ex 1. 12 + 18 ÷ 6 × 3 − 4 = ?
18 ÷ 6 = 3; 3 × 3 = 9; 12 + 9 − 4 = 17.
Ex 2. Convert 0.4545… to a fraction.
Two digits repeat: 45/99 = 5/11.
Ex 3. 46 × 5 = ?
Half of 46 = 23, ×10 = 230.

5. Expanded lesson: Fractions and order of operations

Use BODMAS carefully: brackets, powers/roots, division and multiplication from left to right, then addition and subtraction from left to right. For fraction division, multiply by the reciprocal. Reduce fractions by cancelling common factors.
Additional solved example 1.
Evaluate 3/4 + 5/6.
Solution: LCM 12: 9/12 + 10/12 = 19/12 = 1 7/12.
Additional solved example 2.
Evaluate 2.4 ÷ 0.06.
Solution: Multiply both values by 100: 240 ÷ 6 = 40.
