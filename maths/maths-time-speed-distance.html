Maths: Time, Speed & Distance, Trains, Boats

RRB Group D / NTPC / ALP / JE • Concept, tricks, solved examples, practice

1. Formulas & concepts

  • Distance = Speed × Time
  • km/h → m/s: × 5/18; m/s → km/h: × 18/5
  • Average speed for equal distances = 2xy/(x + y)
  • Train crossing a pole = length/speed; crossing a platform = (L + P)/speed
  • Two trains: opposite directions add speeds; same direction subtract
  • Boat: downstream = u + v; upstream = u − v; still water = (D + U)/2; stream = (D − U)/2
  • Same distance: ratio of speeds = inverse ratio of times

2. Memory tricks

  • Multiples of 18: 18 km/h = 5 m/s, 36 = 10, 54 = 15, 72 = 20, 90 = 25.
  • "Towards = add, behind = subtract" for relative speed.
  • Boat: D is the larger (Down), U the smaller (Up). Stream = half the gap.
  • Average speed is never the plain average when distances are equal. Use 2xy/(x+y).
  • Train problems: the distance to cover is always train length (+ other object length).

3. Solved examples

Ex 1. Convert 72 km/h to m/s.
72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s.
Ex 2. A 150 m train at 54 km/h crosses a 150 m platform. Time?
54 km/h = 15 m/s. (150 + 150)/15 = 20 s.
Ex 3. Equal distance at 40 km/h and 60 km/h. Average speed?
2 × 40 × 60/(40 + 60) = 48 km/h.

4. Practice (tap to see answer)

Q1. 90 km/h in m/s?

(a) 20 (b) 25 (c) 30 (d) 35

Answer: (b) 25 m/s

Q2. Trains 110 m and 100 m run opposite at 72 and 54 km/h. Time to cross each other?

(a) 5 s (b) 6 s (c) 7 s (d) 8 s

Answer: (b) 6 s (relative 126 km/h = 35 m/s; 210/35)

Q3. Still-water speed 12 km/h, stream 3 km/h. Time to go 45 km downstream?

(a) 3 h (b) 4 h (c) 5 h (d) 3.75 h

Answer: (a) 3 h (speed 15)

Q4. Speeds in ratio 3:4 for the same distance. Ratio of times?

(a) 3:4 (b) 4:3 (c) 9:16 (d) 1:1

Answer: (b) 4:3

Q5. A car at 60 km/h covers 150 km in?

(a) 2 h (b) 2.5 h (c) 3 h (d) 3.5 h

Answer: (b) 2.5 h

5. Expanded lesson: Relative speed and average speed

For opposite directions, relative speed is the sum. For the same direction, it is the difference. For equal distances at speeds u and v, average speed is 2uv/(u+v).
Additional solved example 1.
Convert 90 km/h to m/s.
Solution: 90 × 5/18 = 25/2 = 12.5 m/s.
Additional solved example 2.
A 180 m train crosses a pole at 15 m/s. Time?
Solution: 180/15 = 12 seconds.
