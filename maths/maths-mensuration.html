Maths: Mensuration (2D & 3D)

RRB Group D / NTPC / ALP / JE • Concept, tricks, solved examples, practice

1. Formulas & concepts

  • Rectangle: area lb, perimeter 2(l+b) | Square: a², 4a | Triangle: ½bh | Equilateral: (√3/4)a² | Parallelogram: bh | Rhombus: ½d₁d₂ | Trapezium: ½(a+b)h
  • Heron: area = √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)], s = (a+b+c)/2
  • Circle: πr², 2πr
  • Cube: V = a³, TSA = 6a² | Cuboid: V = lbh, TSA = 2(lb+bh+hl)
  • Cylinder: V = πr²h, CSA = 2πrh, TSA = 2πr(r+h)
  • Cone: V = ⅓πr²h, CSA = πrl, l² = r² + h²
  • Sphere: V = 4/3 πr³, SA = 4πr² | Hemisphere: V = 2/3 πr³, CSA = 2πr², TSA = 3πr²

2. Memory tricks

  • Cone : Hemisphere : Cylinder = 1 : 2 : 3 (same r and h = r).
  • Use π = 22/7 when radius or diameter is a multiple of 7.
  • Units: area in m², volume in m³.
  • Hemisphere TSA = 3πr² (curved 2πr² + base πr²).
  • Common triangles 13-14-15 (area 84), 5-12-13 (area 30), 3-4-5 (area 6).

3. Solved examples

Ex 1. Area of a triangle with sides 13, 14, 15?
s = 21. Area = √(21×8×7×6) = √7056 = 84.
Ex 2. Cylinder r = 7 cm, h = 10 cm. Volume and CSA?
V = 22/7 × 49 × 10 = 1540 cm³. CSA = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 10 = 440 cm².
Ex 3. A cube has TSA 150 cm². Volume?
6a² = 150, a = 5. V = 125 cm³.

4. Practice (tap to see answer)

Q1. Area of a circle of radius 14?

(a) 308 (b) 616 (c) 154 (d) 44

Answer: (b) 616

Q2. Rectangle perimeter 36, length 10. Area?

(a) 72 (b) 80 (c) 90 (d) 100

Answer: (b) 80 (breadth = 8)

Q3. Volume of a cube with edge 6?

(a) 36 (b) 108 (c) 216 (d) 256

Answer: (c) 216

Q4. Surface area of a sphere of radius 7?

(a) 308 (b) 462 (c) 616 (d) 1232

Answer: (c) 616

Q5. Cone r = 3, h = 4. Volume?

(a) 12π (b) 15π (c) 36π (d) 9π

Answer: (a) 12π (⅓ × 9 × 4)

5. Expanded lesson: Composite figures and unit conversion

Break a composite shape into familiar rectangles, triangles and circles. Add areas for joined regions and subtract cut-out regions. Convert all dimensions to the same unit before applying a formula.
Additional solved example 1.
Find the area of a triangle with base 14 cm and height 9 cm.
Solution: Area = ½ × 14 × 9 = 63 cm².
Additional solved example 2.
A cube has side 6 cm. Find its volume and total surface area.
Solution: Volume = 6³ = 216 cm³; TSA = 6 × 6² = 216 cm².
