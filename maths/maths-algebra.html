Maths: Algebra

RRB Group D / NTPC / ALP / JE • Concept, tricks, solved examples, practice

1. Formulas & concepts

  • (a+b)³ = a³ + b³ + 3ab(a+b); a³ + b³ = (a+b)(a² − ab + b²); a³ − b³ = (a−b)(a² + ab + b²)
  • If a + b + c = 0 then a³ + b³ + c³ = 3abc
  • a² + b² = (a+b)² − 2ab; if x + 1/x = k then x² + 1/x² = k² − 2
  • Quadratic ax² + bx + c = 0: sum of roots = −b/a, product = c/a
  • Linear equation: move variables to one side, numbers to the other

2. Memory tricks

  • SOAP for the second bracket of a³ ± b³: Same sign as the first bracket, Opposite sign in the middle, Always Positive at the end.
  • Sum zero → cubes = 3abc (the most repeated question).
  • x + 1/x: square both sides and subtract 2 to get x² + 1/x². For x − 1/x, square and add 2.
  • Product and sum of roots: for x² − (sum)x + (product) = 0.

3. Solved examples

Ex 1. x + 1/x = 5. Find x² + 1/x².
5² − 2 = 23.
Ex 2. a + b + c = 0 and abc = 4. Find a³ + b³ + c³.
3abc = 3 × 4 = 12.
Ex 3. Solve x² − 5x + 6 = 0.
(x − 2)(x − 3) = 0, so x = 2 or 3.

4. Practice (tap to see answer)

Q1. 101² − 99² = ?

(a) 200 (b) 300 (c) 400 (d) 404

Answer: (c) 400 (200 × 2)

Q2. a + b = 7 and ab = 12. a² + b² = ?

(a) 19 (b) 25 (c) 37 (d) 49

Answer: (b) 25 (49 − 24)

Q3. Sum of roots of x² − 7x + 10 = 0?

(a) 5 (b) −7 (c) 7 (d) 10

Answer: (c) 7

Q4. x − 1/x = 3. x² + 1/x² = ?

(a) 7 (b) 9 (c) 11 (d) 13

Answer: (c) 11 (9 + 2)

Q5. 2x + 3 = 11. x = ?

(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 7

Answer: (b) 4

5. Expanded lesson: Equations and identities

When solving an equation, perform the same operation on both sides. For factorisation, look for two numbers whose product is the constant term and whose sum is the coefficient of x in x²+bx+c.
Additional solved example 1.
Solve 4x − 9 = 23.
Solution: 4x = 32, so x = 8.
Additional solved example 2.
Factorise x² + 7x + 12.
Solution: (x+3)(x+4).
