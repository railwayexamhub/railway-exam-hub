Maths: Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns

RRB Group D / NTPC / ALP / JE • Concept, tricks, solved examples, practice

1. Formulas & concepts

  • Work = Rate × Time; Rate = 1/time
  • A takes x days, B takes y days: together = xy/(x + y)
  • M₁D₁H₁/W₁ = M₂D₂H₂/W₂
  • Efficiency is inversely proportional to time
  • Pipes: inlet = +, outlet/leak = −; net rate = sum of rates
  • Wages are shared in the ratio of work done (efficiency × days)

2. Memory tricks

  • LCM method: take total work = LCM of the days. Convert each person to units per day. Time = total ÷ combined units.
  • "More men, fewer days": men and days are inverse; men and work are direct.
  • Pipes = work: filling is +, emptying is −. Use the same LCM method.
  • Two people, product over sum: 12 and 20 days → 240/32 = 7.5 days.

3. Solved examples

Ex 1. A can do a job in 10 days, B in 15 days. Together?
LCM = 30 units. A = 3/day, B = 2/day, total 5/day. 30/5 = 6 days.
Ex 2. A, B, C take 12, 18, 36 days. Together?
LCM = 36. Rates 3 + 2 + 1 = 6/day. 36/6 = 6 days.
Ex 3. A + B finish in 8 days; A alone in 12 days. B alone?
LCM = 24. A + B = 3/day, A = 2/day, so B = 1/day. 24 days.

4. Practice (tap to see answer)

Q1. A: 20 days, B: 30 days. Together?

(a) 10 (b) 12 (c) 15 (d) 25

Answer: (b) 12 days

Q2. A does half the work in 4 days; B finishes the other half in 6 days. B alone takes?

(a) 10 (b) 12 (c) 15 (d) 18

Answer: (b) 12 days

Q3. A pipe fills a tank in 6 h; a leak empties it in 12 h. Both open, time to fill?

(a) 8 (b) 10 (c) 12 (d) 18

Answer: (c) 12 hours

Q4. 12 men finish a job in 10 days. 15 men finish in?

(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 9 (d) 12

Answer: (b) 8 days

Q5. A and B take 8 days together; A alone 12 days. B alone?

(a) 20 (b) 24 (c) 30 (d) 36

Answer: (b) 24 days

5. Expanded lesson: Work-rate method

If a person completes a job in d days, the one-day work rate is 1/d. Add rates for people working together. Subtract an outlet's rate from an inlet's rate in tank problems.
Additional solved example 1.
A completes a job in 12 days and B in 18 days. Together?
Solution: Rate=1/12+1/18=5/36; time=36/5=7.2 days.
Additional solved example 2.
A pipe fills in 5 hours and a leak empties in 20 hours. Net filling time?
Solution: Net rate=1/5−1/20=3/20; time=20/3 hours.
