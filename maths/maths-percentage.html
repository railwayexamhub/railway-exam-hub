Maths: Percentage

RRB Group D / NTPC / ALP / JE • Concepts, shortcuts, solved examples, practice

1. Formulas & concepts

  • Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100
  • x% of y = (x × y) ÷ 100
  • To convert a fraction to percentage, multiply by 100; to convert percentage to fraction, divide by 100 and simplify.
  • Increase percentage = (Increase ÷ Original value) × 100.
  • Decrease percentage = (Decrease ÷ Original value) × 100.
  • After x% increase: New value = Original × (100 + x) ÷ 100.
  • After x% decrease: New value = Original × (100 − x) ÷ 100.
  • Successive changes a% and b%: net percentage change = a + b + (ab ÷ 100), using negative values for decreases.
  • If A is x% more than B, then B is [x ÷ (100+x)] × 100% less than A.

2. Memory tricks

  • 10%: move the decimal one place left; 1%: move it two places left; 5%: half of 10%.
  • 25% = one-fourth; 20% = one-fifth; 12.5% = one-eighth; 50% = one-half.
  • For successive percentage changes, do not simply add the percentages; include the product term.

3. Solved examples

Example 1: Find 25% of 360. Solution: 360 × 25 ÷ 100 = 90.

Example 2: A value rises from 400 to 460. Percentage increase = 60 ÷ 400 × 100 = 15%.

Example 3: A price of ₹800 is reduced by 10%. New price = 800 × 90 ÷ 100 = ₹720.

Example 4: A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 10%. Net change = 20 − 10 − 2 = 8% increase.

4. Practice (tap to see answer)

Q1. What is 15% of 240?

(a) 24 (b) 30 (c) 36 (d) 40
Answer: (c) 36

Q2. 40 is what percentage of 160?

(a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 30% (d) 40%
Answer: (b) 25%

Q3. A value of 500 increases by 12%. New value?

(a) 550 (b) 555 (c) 560 (d) 565
Answer: (c) 560

Q4. A number decreases from 800 to 680. Decrease percentage?

(a) 10% (b) 12% (c) 15% (d) 20%
Answer: (c) 15%

Q5. A price increases by 10% and then decreases by 10%. Net result?

(a) No change (b) 1% loss (c) 1% gain (d) 2% loss
Answer: (b) 1% loss

5. Expanded lesson: Successive percentage changes

Use the original value as the base for a single percentage change. For successive changes, apply each percentage to the updated value, or use a+b+ab/100 with decreases entered as negative values.
Additional solved example 1.
A salary of ₹20,000 increases by 10%, then 5%. Find final salary.
Solution: 20,000×1.10×1.05 = ₹23,100.
Additional solved example 2.
A price falls from ₹800 to ₹680. Find decrease percentage.
Solution: Decrease=₹120; 120/800×100=15%.
