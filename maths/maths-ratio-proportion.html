Maths: Ratio, Proportion, Partnership & Mixture

RRB Group D / NTPC / ALP / JE • Concept, tricks, solved examples, practice

1. Formulas & concepts

  • a : b = a/b; a:b = c:d ⇒ ad = bc
  • Mean proportional of a and b = √(ab); third proportional of a and b = b²/a
  • Joining ratios: a:b and b:c → make the common term equal
  • Partnership: profit share ∝ capital × time
  • Alligation: cheaper qty : dearer qty = (dearer − mean) : (mean − cheaper)
  • Componendo-dividendo: a/b = c/d ⇒ (a+b)/(a−b) = (c+d)/(c−d)

2. Memory tricks

  • Alligation = cross-subtract. Mean in the centre, subtract diagonally; answers give the ratio.
  • Extremes × means: in a:b = c:d, ad = bc.
  • Partnership: equal time → share by capital; equal capital → share by time; otherwise multiply capital × time.
  • Divide in a ratio: one part = total ÷ sum of ratio terms.

3. Solved examples

Ex 1. Divide ₹900 in the ratio 2:3:4.
Sum = 9, one part = 100. Shares: ₹200, ₹300, ₹400.
Ex 2. Mean proportional of 4 and 25?
√(4 × 25) = 10.
Ex 3. Sugar at ₹40/kg and ₹50/kg is mixed to get ₹46/kg. Ratio?
(50 − 46) : (46 − 40) = 4 : 6 = 2 : 3.

4. Practice (tap to see answer)

Q1. a:b = 2:3 and b:c = 4:5. Find a:b:c.

(a) 8:12:15 (b) 2:3:5 (c) 6:8:10 (d) 4:6:5

Answer: (a) 8:12:15

Q2. Third proportional of 4 and 6?

(a) 8 (b) 9 (c) 10 (d) 12

Answer: (b) 9 (36/4)

Q3. A invests ₹3000 for 12 months, B ₹4000 for 6 months. Profit ₹700. A's share?

(a) 280 (b) 350 (c) 420 (d) 450

Answer: (c) ₹420 (36000 : 24000 = 3:2)

Q4. Ages in ratio 3:5, sum 40. Elder's age?

(a) 15 (b) 20 (c) 25 (d) 30

Answer: (c) 25

Q5. x:y = 3:4. (x+y):(y−x) = ?

(a) 7:1 (b) 1:7 (c) 3:4 (d) 7:4

Answer: (a) 7:1

5. Expanded lesson: Direct and inverse proportion

In direct proportion, y/x remains constant. In inverse proportion, x×y remains constant. For partnership, each partner's share is proportional to investment multiplied by the time invested.
Additional solved example 1.
Divide ₹960 in the ratio 5:7.
Solution: Total parts=12. Shares = ₹400 and ₹560.
Additional solved example 2.
Eight workers finish a job in 15 days. How long for 12 workers?
Solution: 8 × 15 = 12 × days, so 10 days.
