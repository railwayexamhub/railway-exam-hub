Simple and Compound Interest

RRB Group D Mathematics • Chapter 8

About this chapter

Interest is the cost of borrowing or the return earned on money invested.

Study tip: Read the rules, practise the worked examples, then attempt the quiz without looking at the notes.

1. Key concepts and rules

  • Principal (P) is the original amount.
  • Rate (R) is the annual interest rate in percent.
  • Time (T) must be expressed in years when using annual rate.
  • Compound interest is calculated on principal plus accumulated interest.

2. Important formulas and reminders

Simple Interest (SI) = (P × R × T)/100
Amount under SI = P + SI
Amount under annual CI = P(1+R/100)^T
CI = Amount − P

3. Solved examples

Example 1: Find SI on ₹2,000 at 5% for 3 years.

SI = 2000×5×3/100.

Answer: ₹300

Example 2: Find amount on ₹1,000 at 10% compound interest for 2 years.

Amount = 1000×1.1×1.1.

Answer: ₹1,210

Example 3: Find CI on ₹5,000 at 10% for 1 year, compounded annually.

For one year, CI = 5000×10/100.

Answer: ₹500

4. Exam tips

  • Write down the given values before selecting a formula.
  • Check that units are consistent.
  • Do calculations step by step and simplify the final answer.
  • Estimate the result to catch arithmetic mistakes.
  • Practise questions under a time limit after learning the method.
