Simple and Compound Interest

RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 08

About this chapter

Principal, rate, time, simple interest and annual compound interest. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • Simple Interest (SI) = (P × R × T)/100.
  • Amount under SI = P + SI.
  • Compound Amount (annual) = P(1 + R/100)^T.
  • Compound Interest = Amount − Principal.
  • Keep rate and time units consistent.

Important formulas and reminders

Simple Interest (SI) = (P × R × T)/100.
Amount under SI = P + SI.

Solved examples

Practice concept 1

Find SI on ₹1,000 at 5% per year for 2 years.

Answer: ₹100

SI = 1000 × 5 × 2 / 100 = ₹100.

Practice concept 2

Find the amount on ₹1,000 at 10% compound interest for 1 year.

Answer: ₹1,100

For one year, interest = 10% of 1000 = ₹100. Amount = ₹1,100.

Practice concept 3

What is the SI formula?

Answer: (P × R × T)/100

Simple interest is principal multiplied by annual rate and time, divided by 100.
