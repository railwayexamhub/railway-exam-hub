Geometry

RRB Group D Mathematics • Chapter 16

About this chapter

Geometry studies points, lines, angles, triangles, quadrilaterals and circles.

Study tip: Read the rules, practise the worked examples, then attempt the quiz without looking at the notes.

1. Key concepts and rules

  • A right angle is 90°; a straight angle is 180°; a full turn is 360°.
  • Angles on a straight line add to 180°.
  • Angles around a point add to 360°.
  • The interior angles of a triangle add to 180°.
  • In an isosceles triangle, base angles are equal.

2. Important formulas and reminders

Triangle angle sum = 180°
Quadrilateral interior angle sum = 360°
Sum of interior angles of an n-sided polygon = (n−2)×180°
Each exterior angle of a regular n-sided polygon = 360°/n

3. Solved examples

Example 1: Two angles of a triangle are 50° and 60°. Find the third.

Third angle = 180−50−60.

Answer: 70°

Example 2: Find each interior angle of a square.

A square has four right angles.

Answer: 90°

Example 3: Find the sum of interior angles of a pentagon.

(5−2)×180.

Answer: 540°

4. Exam tips

  • Write down the given values before selecting a formula.
  • Check that units are consistent.
  • Do calculations step by step and simplify the final answer.
  • Estimate the result to catch arithmetic mistakes.
  • Practise questions under a time limit after learning the method.
