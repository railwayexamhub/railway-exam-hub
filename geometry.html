Geometry

RRB Group D Mathematics · Chapter 16

About this chapter

Lines, angles, triangles, quadrilaterals, circles and basic geometric properties. This chapter page is designed for online revision and practice on Railway Exam Hub.

Key concepts and rules

  • Angles on a straight line add to 180°.
  • Angles around a point add to 360°.
  • The interior angles of a triangle add to 180°.
  • A right angle is 90°.
  • Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral add to 180°.

Solved examples

Practice concept 1

Two angles of a triangle are 50° and 60°. Find the third angle.

Answer: 70°

Third angle = 180° − 50° − 60° = 70°.

Practice concept 2

A right angle measures:

Answer: 90°

A right angle is exactly 90 degrees.

Practice concept 3

Angles on a straight line total:

Answer: 180°
